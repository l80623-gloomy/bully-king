## 二、BG11 住家夜晚角落

### 畫面設計

- 安靜整潔的居家工作角落，以暖桌燈和深藍夜景形成冷暖對比。
- 桌面配置空白筆記本、筆、手機與工作包；物件只暗示整理思緒，不呈現特定案件內容。
- 中央桌椅置於中後景，前景及左右保留角色疊放空間。
- 情緒設定為安全、沉靜、可思考，不使用恐怖、懲罰、自責或自傷暗示。

### 最終提示詞

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: landscape background for the workplace anti-bullying interactive game 《霸凌王》
Primary request: Create BG11, a quiet corner of a Taiwanese employee's home at night, used for reflection and organizing thoughts before seeking help.
Input images: Existing BG10 and BG07 are style-only references. Match their layered cut-paper collage, paper fibers, softly raised edges, gentle shadows, simplified geometric shapes, muted teal, navy and warm beige palette. Do not copy their layouts.
Scene/backdrop: A modest tidy home corner at night with a wooden desk, warm lamp, face-down smartphone, closed work bag, blank notebook and pen, simple chair, plant, and calm deep-blue city view.
Composition/framing: Wide 3:2 landscape at natural eye level. Keep a broad clear foreground and usable standing space on both sides for character overlays.
Lighting/mood: Warm lamp glow balanced by cool window light; calm, supportive and emotionally safe.
Constraints: No people, silhouettes, readable text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark or speech bubbles. Notebook and phone screen remain blank. No dramatic mess, alcohol, medication or self-harm cues. Empty reusable background only.
```

## 三、BG12 辦公室走廊與門口

### 畫面設計

- 乾淨安靜的辦公室走廊，以半掩木門、磨砂玻璃和等候長椅建立「尚未進入」的決策時刻。
- 門內僅露出空椅與柔和暖光，不出現受理人、調查結果或安慰行為。
- 前景留白，可讓後續角色站在門外、長椅旁或走廊兩側。
- 場景維持親近、保密與可求助感，不做成醫療院所、警衛空間或壓迫性機構。

### 最終提示詞

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: landscape background for the workplace anti-bullying interactive game 《霸凌王》
Primary request: Create BG12, an office corridor and doorway outside a private consultation or grievance room, suitable for a character pausing before entering or waiting.
Input images: Existing BG07 and BG10 are style-only references. Match their layered cut-paper collage, paper fibers, softly raised edges, gentle shadows, simplified geometric shapes and muted sage green, charcoal and warm beige palette. Do not copy their layouts.
Scene/backdrop: A calm Taiwanese office corridor with one partially open plain wooden door, softly lit private room, frosted-glass wall panel, waiting bench and small potted plant. Through the opening, show only an empty chair and warm light.
Composition/framing: Wide 3:2 landscape at eye level. Keep generous clear floor and standing areas on both sides. Doorway sits slightly off-center with a readable path toward it.
Lighting/mood: Soft daylight with reassuring warm light from the room; calm, discreet and approachable without promising an outcome.
Constraints: No people or silhouettes behind glass, readable text, room numbers, signs, letters, logos, watermark or speech bubbles. No completed consultation, investigation result, security camera, locked door or threatening atmosphere. Empty reusable background only.
```
