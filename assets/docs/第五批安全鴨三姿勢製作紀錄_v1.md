## 二、製作調整

初次提示版與完成版雖成功建立造型，但工具加入黃黑背景光暈，不適合作為透明立繪。警示版的造型與邊界較乾淨，因此改以警示版作為正式角色錨點，重新生成提示與完成姿勢。未採用的光暈版本未放入正式素材目錄。

三張採用的生成原稿仍把棋盤格畫入像素，故保留於 `assets/archive/`，再以本機流程辨識背景、保留最大人物連通區並柔化邊緣。正式圖已使用深藍、米白與綠色三種底色檢查安全帽、眼白、翅膀羽緣與蹼足。

## 三、最終提示詞

### 提示姿勢

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-body reusable UI mascot cutout for a workplace anti-bullying interactive game
Primary request: Create the hint/guide pose of the exact same 「安全鴨」 from the reference. Preserve exactly the same round head, yellow body proportions, orange beak and feet, dark eyes, deep navy segmented safety helmet, sage-green neckerchief, layered feather shapes and paper texture. Change only the pose and direction.
Pose and direction: full body including both feet; body turned three-quarter toward the viewer's right, one wing gently pointing toward empty space on the right, the other wing relaxed near the torso; curious encouraging expression and slight head tilt; both feet planted with unequal weight.
Composition: portrait 2:3 canvas, mascot about 70 percent of canvas height, slightly left of center with generous empty space on the right.
Style/medium: handcrafted layered cut-paper collage identical to reference, fine fibers, clean edges, shallow layer shadows only.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with alpha; if transparency cannot be shown, use only a plain neutral checkerboard-like transparency preview with no texture. Absolutely no black background, colored glow, halo, spotlight, gradient, floor, cast shadow outside the character, text, letters, numbers, logo, badge, speech bubble, watermark, extra objects or extra characters. Do not redesign the mascot.
```

### 警示姿勢

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: second full-body reusable UI mascot cutout for a workplace anti-bullying interactive game
Primary request: Create the warning pose of the exact same fictional 「安全鴨」 shown in the reference. Preserve the same rounded yellow duck body, head-to-body ratio, orange beak and webbed feet, large dark eyes, deep navy plain safety helmet, sage-green neckerchief, paper textures, proportions and facial construction. This must unmistakably be the same mascot, not a redesign.
Pose and direction: full body including both feet; body turned three-quarter toward the viewer's left; left wing extended with a gentle open-palm stop gesture, right wing lightly touching the side of the safety helmet; feet set in a stable wider stance; focused, calm expression with closed beak, not frightened or angry.
Style/medium: handcrafted layered cut-paper collage, fine paper fibers, clean paper edges and shallow shadows between body layers; match the reference exactly, not photorealistic or plastic 3D.
Composition: portrait 2:3 canvas, mascot occupying roughly 70 percent of height, positioned slightly right of center with generous empty space on the left for warning UI.
Color palette: identical warm yellow, orange, deep navy and muted sage from the reference.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with alpha. Absolutely no black backdrop, glow, halo, spotlight, gradient, floor, cast shadow outside the character, checkerboard pattern, text, letters, numbers, logo, badge, speech bubble, warning sign, watermark, extra objects or extra characters.
```

### 完成姿勢

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-body reusable UI mascot cutout
Primary request: Keep this exact same 「安全鴨」 design and render a completion pose on the same clean transparency-preview treatment as the reference image. Change only the pose: front-facing small hop, one foot gently lifted, both wings spread outward, relaxed pleased face with beak slightly open.
Invariants: identical round head and body proportions, yellow layered feathers, orange beak and webbed feet, deep navy segmented safety helmet, sage-green neckerchief, eye shape, paper texture and cut-paper craft style.
Composition: portrait 2:3, whole helmet, both wing tips and both feet fully visible, character centered and about 68 percent of canvas height with clear margins.
Background requirement: actual transparent alpha preferred; otherwise use the same flat neutral gray-and-white transparency preview seen in the reference. No dark backdrop, colored glow, halo, spotlight, gradient, floor, external shadow or scenery.
Avoid: confetti, trophy, medal, words, letters, numbers, logos, badges, speech bubbles, watermark, extra objects, extra characters, redesign, photorealism, glossy 3D.
```
