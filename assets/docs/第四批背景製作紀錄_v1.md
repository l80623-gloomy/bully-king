## 二、BG08 董事會議室

### 使用目的

支援升遷評估、正式彙報、基層意見進入決策層等制度與文化事件。構圖以長桌主位、座位距離與視線方向呈現正式決策空間，但不將高階主管預設為反派。

### 最終提示詞

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: reusable landscape background for a workplace anti-bullying interactive game
Primary request: Create BG08, an empty executive boardroom / directors' meeting room in the exact layered cut-paper collage visual language of the two reference backgrounds.
Scene/backdrop: A formal but restrained modern boardroom, with a long meeting table, multiple empty chairs, a clearly readable head-of-table position, tall windows with a simplified Taiwanese urban skyline, and a clean blank presentation wall. Include only a few closed folders and abstract paper sheets with no markings.
Style/medium: handcrafted layered paper collage, visible fine paper fibers, simplified geometric silhouettes, gently beveled paper edges and soft shallow shadows; consistent with the reference images, not photorealistic, not 3D plastic.
Composition/framing: wide 3:2 landscape game background; eye-level view; leave generous open floor/foreground and usable negative space on both sides for later character cutouts and dialogue UI. The table and seating should communicate formal decision-making through distance and sightlines without feeling intimidating or luxurious.
Lighting/mood: calm daytime natural light, professional and neutral, psychologically safe rather than ominous.
Color palette: muted sage green, warm beige, charcoal navy and dusty blue, matching the project palette.
Constraints: absolutely no people; no readable words, letters, numbers, charts, company logos, flags, emblems, nameplates, watermarks, or visible screen content. No dramatic villain lighting. No embedded title. Deliver one polished full-frame background image.
```

### 檢查結果

- 主位、長桌及座位秩序清楚，可支援權力距離與程序討論。
- 前景及左右兩側仍有角色與介面配置空間。
- 無人物、可讀文字、公司識別或可辨識螢幕資訊。
- 色彩、紙紋與既有背景一致；氛圍正式但不陰沉。

## 三、BG09 尾牙與部門聚餐

### 使用目的

支援團隊活動、非正式交流、參與壓力與座位互動。畫面保留空椅、走道與出口，讓後續角色配置可以呈現參與、婉拒、替代方案或陪伴等不同選擇。

### 最終提示詞

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: reusable landscape background for a workplace anti-bullying interactive game
Primary request: Create BG09, an empty company year-end dinner / department gathering venue in the exact layered cut-paper collage visual language of the two reference backgrounds.
Scene/backdrop: A warm, modest restaurant-style gathering space with two round communal dining tables, empty chairs, simple neutral table settings, soft pendant lights, a small unbranded side area suggesting an activity corner, and a clearly visible aisle and exit route. The event could be a casual team dinner or year-end gathering, but must not depend on a specific season or holiday.
Style/medium: handcrafted layered paper collage, visible fine paper fibers, simplified geometric silhouettes, gently beveled paper edges and soft shallow shadows; consistent with the reference images, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D.
Composition/framing: wide 3:2 landscape game background; eye-level view; leave generous open foreground and clear side spaces for later character cutouts and dialogue UI. Keep seating arrangements readable, with some chairs slightly separated so later scenes can communicate choice and participation without forcing a conclusion.
Lighting/mood: warm, welcoming evening interior, socially active potential but currently calm and empty; not festive overload.
Color palette: muted sage green, warm beige, dusty terracotta, charcoal navy and soft amber, harmonious with the project palette.
Constraints: absolutely no people; no readable words, letters, numbers, banners, couplets, calendars, company logos, brand marks, alcohol bottles, forced-drinking cues, karaoke lyrics, watermarks, or embedded title. No visible food brand labels. Deliver one polished full-frame background image.
```

### 檢查結果

- 兩組圓桌、空椅、活動角落與出口動線清楚。
- 前景留白充足，可放置多人角色及互動選項。
- 無人物、可讀文字、品牌、酒類焦點或強迫飲酒暗示。
- 未綁定特定年份或節慶，可重複用於聚餐與團隊活動。
