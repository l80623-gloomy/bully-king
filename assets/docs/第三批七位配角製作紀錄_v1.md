## 二、共用生成提示詞

七次生成均使用下列共用骨架，再加入第三節的個別角色設定：

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-body transparent NPC cutout for the workplace learning game 《霸凌王》
Input images: Existing approved character cutouts are style-only references for proportions, facial simplification, layered paper texture and polished finish. Do not copy their identity, face, outfit, hairstyle, pose or prop.
Style/medium: Handcrafted layered cut-paper collage, visible fine paper fibers, clean geometric silhouette, softly raised paper edges and subtle shadows, consistent with the existing character assets.
Composition: Portrait 2:3, one adult character only, entire body visible from hair to both complete shoes, centered with generous transparent margin, no crop.
Constraints: Genuinely transparent alpha background; no baked checkerboard, floor, scene, readable text, name label, logo or watermark. Keep the expression usable across multiple story outcomes and avoid victim or villain stereotypes.
```

## 三、七位角色的最終提示設定

### 3.1 雅婷

```text
Create a capable Taiwanese female sales professional in her early 30s: oval face, short straight black bob tucked behind one ear, calm attentive expression. Muted petrol-blue tailored blazer, warm ivory blouse, ankle-length charcoal trousers, burgundy low-heel loafers, and one slim olive-green portfolio/tablet. Upright relaxed stance with one free hand slightly open. Clearly different from 小美: no ponytail, red cardigan or mug; professional and emotionally neutral, not crying, timid or victim-coded.
```

### 3.2 阿柱

```text
Create a seasoned Taiwanese male master technician in his late 50s: broad round face, short swept-back silver-gray hair, gentle crow's-feet, no glasses or moustache. Burnt-orange work overshirt with rolled cuffs, dark slate T-shirt, sturdy khaki work trousers, brown safety shoes and compact dark tool pouch. Hold one closed adjustable wrench safely downward. Experienced, steady and willing to listen; no dangerous action, raised tool, anger or villain face. Clearly different from 林班長.
```

### 3.3 阿仁

```text
Create an Indonesian male production colleague around age 30: warm medium-brown skin, long oval face, short neat wavy black hair with a side part, clean-shaven, friendly observant expression. Muted maroon work jacket, sand crew-neck shirt, dark olive work trousers and navy safety shoes. Carry a small cobalt-blue reusable lunch bag. Respectful individual portrayal with no ethnic caricature. Clearly different from 阿迪: no yellow reflective vest or blue work shirt, and a different face and hairstyle.
```

### 3.4 小凱

```text
Create a Taiwanese male production newcomer age 23–26: slim-to-average build, rounded rectangular face, neat straight dark hair with short side fringe, alert but slightly uncertain expression. Dusty sky-blue zip work jacket, cream T-shirt, beige work trousers and dark teal safety shoes. Hold a small blank gray-blue training manual, with the other hand ready to ask a question. Capable learner rather than helpless; no tears, injury, humiliation or victim stereotype. Distinct from 小宏 and 阿宏.
```

### 3.5 夏經理

```text
Create a Taiwanese male department manager in his late 40s who can appear in both an upheld complaint and an exoneration storyline: average build, balanced oval-square face, neatly side-parted salt-and-pepper hair, thin dark rectangular glasses and composed neutral expression. Deep navy unstructured blazer, pale-blue open-collar shirt, medium-gray trousers and dark brown dress shoes. Hold a closed charcoal document folio. No villain face, clenched fist, smug grin, tears or predetermined guilt; professional without luxury styling.
```

### 3.6 阿政

```text
Create a Taiwanese male HR newcomer in his mid-to-late 20s: lean build, soft round face, tidy short dark hair, small round glasses and thoughtful attentive expression. Light sage-green button-up shirt with rolled sleeves, muted navy knitted vest, taupe trousers and dark loafers. Hold a blue-gray folder with blank colored tabs, blank sticky-note pad and pen. Organized but still learning; no childish student look, panic, crying or fixed victim posture. Distinct from the female 人資窗口 and 小葳.
```

### 3.7 建宏

```text
Create a confident Taiwanese male line leader in his early 40s: athletic-average build, angular oval face, very short neatly cropped black hair, no glasses and clean-shaven. Sand-tan structured work jacket, dark indigo polo, graphite work trousers and black safety shoes. Hold a slate-blue scheduling clipboard with blank sheets and use a small open explanatory gesture. Confident and cooperative rather than dominant; no rank insignia, hard hat, aggression or villain styling. Distinct from 徐課長、林班長、阿柱 and 小杰.
```

## 四、本機透明處理

生成結果分為兩類：

1. 雅婷、阿仁、小凱為 RGB 圖，畫面中的棋盤格是實際像素。先以既有本機工具分割，但 v1 仍殘留背景，因此保留於 `assets/archive/`；再改用 GrabCut 另存正式 v2。
2. 阿柱、夏經理、阿政、建宏已有 alpha，但人物周圍帶有低透明彩色光暈。使用 `tools/影像處理/clean_generated_alpha_character.py` 將 alpha 16 以下歸零，再把有效透明範圍重映射至 0～255。

所有生成原稿均保留於 `assets/archive/`，正式檔另存至 `assets/images/characters/`；本機去背與 alpha 清理沒有上傳圖片或下載模型，也沒有覆蓋原始生成檔。
