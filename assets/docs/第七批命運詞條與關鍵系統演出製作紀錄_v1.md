## 二、詞條卡共用最終提示詞

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical Roguelite modifier card for the workplace education game 《霸凌王》
Input images: existing 《霸凌王》 paper-collage images are style references only. Match their handmade layered paper collage, visible paper fibers, irregular cut edges, gentle physical shadows, and restrained mature workplace-training palette.
Style/medium: handcrafted cut-paper collage, tactile layered cardstock, polished game card illustration, not photorealistic, not vector-flat.
Composition/framing: portrait 2:3 full-bleed card artwork, a consistent thick deep-navy outer paper frame and warm-ivory inner frame, one bold central symbol in the upper roughly two-thirds, and a large completely blank warm-ivory torn-paper panel across the bottom roughly one-third for later HTML text.
Color palette: deep navy, warm ivory, sage green, muted gray-purple, brick red, with small soft-yellow accents.
Constraints: no readable text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, decorative border text, or photorealism. The artwork has an opaque paper background.
```

## 五、透明去背與版本紀錄
